
# 👋 About Me

Wenxin Luo (羅雯歆) is a Research Assistant at Lingnan University, supervised by Prof. Daoyuan Wu of the [AIS2 Lab](https://ais2lab.github.io/). She received her M.Sc. in Data Science from City University of Hong Kong, where she conducted research with Prof. Xiangyu Zhao at the [AML Lab](https://aml-cityu.github.io/), and her B.Eng. in Software Engineering from Beijing University of Technology. Her research focuses on large language models, machine learning, and AI security and alignment.
