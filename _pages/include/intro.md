
Wenxin Luo (羅雯歆 in Chinese) is now a a Research Assistant at Lingnan University, supervised by Professor Daoyuan Wu. She research focuses on the security and alignment of large language models, including adversarial evaluation, jailbreak defense, and safety-aware fine-tuning.

She received her MSc in Data Science from City University of Hong Kong and her bachelor degree in Software Engineering from Beijing University of Technology. Before joining Lingnan University, She worked on LLM security, audio-based telecom fraud detection, and task-adaptive prompt optimization at City University of Hong Kong and the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology.

**Research Interests**: Large Language Models, Machine Learning, Safety Alignment, AI Security.
