
# 👋 About Me

Wenxin Luo (羅雯歆) is a Research Assistant at Lingnan University, supervised by Prof. Daoyuan Wu of the [AIS2 Lab](https://ais2lab.github.io/). 
She holds an M.Sc. in Data Science from City University of Hong Kong and a B.Eng. in Software Engineering from Beijing University of Technology. During her master’s studies, she conducted research with Prof. Xiangyu Zhao at the [AML Lab](https://aml-cityu.github.io/).
Her research focuses on large language models, machine learning, and AI security and alignment.
