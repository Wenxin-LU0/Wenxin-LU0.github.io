
Wenxin Luo (羅雯歆 in Chinese) is a Research Assistant at Lingnan University, where she is supervised by Prof. Daoyuan Wu in the [AIS² Lab](https://ais2lab.github.io/). Her research focuses on the security and alignment of large language models, including adversarial evaluation, jailbreak defense, and safety-aware fine-tuning.

Previously, she conducted research with Prof. Xiangyu Zhao in the [Applied Machine Learning Lab (AML Lab)](https://aml-cityu.github.io/) at City University of Hong Kong, working on task-adaptive prompt optimization and hierarchical text classification. Her broader research experience also includes LLM security and audio-based telecom fraud detection at City University of Hong Kong and the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology.

She holds an M.Sc. in Data Science from City University of Hong Kong and a B.Eng. in Software Engineering from Beijing University of Technology.

**Research Interests**: Large Language Models, Machine Learning, Safety Alignment, AI Security.
