# 🧐 About Me

Hi, I am Wenxin Luo. I am a Research Assistant at Lingnan University, supervised by Professor Daoyuan Wu. My research focuses on the security and alignment of large language models, including adversarial evaluation, jailbreak defense, and safety-aware fine-tuning.

I received my M.Sc. in Data Science from City University of Hong Kong and my B.Eng. in Software Engineering from Beijing University of Technology. Before joining Lingnan University, I worked on LLM security, audio-based telecom fraud detection, and task-adaptive prompt optimization at City University of Hong Kong and the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology.

**Research Interests**: Large Language Models, Machine Learning, AI Security, Safety Alignment.
